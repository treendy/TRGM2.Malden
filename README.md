# TRGM2.Malden - Treendy's Randomly Generated Missions 2.0

**Author:** Treendy  
**Version:** 2.0  
**Mission Type:** Cooperative (Coop)  
**Map:** Malden  
**Players:** 5-35

## Overview

TRGM2.Malden is a highly dynamic and replayable mission system for Arma 3 that generates unique military operations each time you play. Every playthrough offers different objectives, enemy placements, weather conditions, and tactical scenarios, ensuring no two missions are ever the same.

## Core Features

### 🎲 **Randomly Generated Missions**
- **Dynamic Objectives**: Each mission randomly selects from 17+ different objective types
- **Variable Enemy Positions**: Enemy forces spawn in different locations with varying compositions
- **Procedural Intel**: Intelligence gathering missions with randomized target locations
- **Adaptive Difficulty**: Mission complexity scales based on team performance

### 🎯 **Mission Types**

The system offers 11 distinct mission type configurations:

1. **Heavy Mission (with two optional sides)** - Main objective with two additional optional side missions
2. **Heavy Mission (two hidden optional sides)** - Main objective with hidden side missions requiring intel
3. **Heavy Mission (two objectives at AO)** - Multiple objectives at the area of operations with random side missions
4. **Heavy Mission (Intel Required)** - Main objective location must be discovered through intelligence gathering
5. **Heavy Mission Only** - Single large-scale operation without side objectives
6. **Single Mission** - Focused single objective operation
7. **Single Mission (two objectives at AO)** - Two objectives at one location with possible side missions
8. **Three Missions** - Three separate objective locations
9. **Three Hidden Missions** - Three missions requiring intelligence to locate
10. **Heavy Hidden Mission (Full Map)** - Large hidden objective anywhere on the map
11. **Campaign Mode** - Persistent progression system with reputation and recruitment

### 📋 **Objective Types**

Players can encounter 17+ different mission objectives:

1. **Download Laptop Data** - Secure intelligence from enemy computers
2. **Steal Research Data** - Extract data from research vehicles
3. **Destroy Ammo Trucks** - Eliminate enemy supply vehicles
4. **Speak with Informant** - Gather intelligence from local sources
5. **Interrogate Officer** - Extract information from captured enemy officers
6. **Bug Radio** - Plant surveillance devices on enemy communications
7. **Eliminate Officer** - Targeted assassination of high-value targets (HVT)
8. **Assassinate Weapon Dealer** - Take out arms trafficking targets
9. **Destroy AAA Vehicles** - Eliminate anti-aircraft threats
10. **Destroy Artillery Vehicles** - Neutralize enemy indirect fire capabilities
11. **Rescue POW** - Extract prisoners of war from enemy custody
12. **Rescue Reporter** - Save journalists from hostile territory
13. **Defuse IEDs** - Explosive ordnance disposal operations
14. **Bomb Mission** - Plant and detonate explosives on strategic targets
15. **Target Mission** - Eliminate specific marked targets
16. **Destroy Cache** - Locate and destroy enemy weapon caches
17. **Secure and Supply** - Capture and hold strategic positions

### 🌍 **Dynamic Events**

Random events that can occur during missions:

- **Downed Helicopter Events** - Crash sites requiring investigation or securing
- **Medical Emergencies** - Civilian casualties requiring assistance
- **Downed Convoy** - Ambushed friendly forces needing rescue
- **Checkpoint Encounters** - Both friendly and hostile checkpoints
- **IED Threats** - Improvised explosive devices on routes
- **AT Mine Fields** - Anti-tank mine obstacles

## Installation

1. Subscribe to the mission on Steam Workshop (if available) or download the mission file
2. Place the mission folder in your Arma 3 missions directory
3. Load the mission in multiplayer mode
4. Configure mission parameters in the lobby
5. Start the mission with 5-35 players

## Getting Started

1. **Mission Selection Screen** - Choose your mission type and parameters
2. **Advanced Settings** - Customize faction, weather, support options
3. **Loadout Selection** - Equip your team via Virtual Arsenal (if enabled)
4. **Briefing** - Review objectives and plan your operation
5. **Mission Start** - Leave HQ area to begin
6. **Complete Objectives** - Follow task markers and gather intel
7. **Return to HQ** - Debrief and receive reputation

## Tips & Strategies

- Gather intelligence before engaging main objectives
- Use UAV reconnaissance to locate hidden targets
- Coordinate with your team for complex objectives
- Manage civilian casualties to maintain reputation
- Call for support when overwhelmed
- Check the mission board regularly in campaign mode
- Use transport helicopters for quick insertion/extraction
